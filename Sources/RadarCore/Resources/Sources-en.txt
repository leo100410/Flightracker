SkyNear 1.1
Primary positions: ADSB.lol, https://www.adsb.lol/
ADSB.lol data: ODbL 1.0, https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
Fallback positions: adsb.fi, https://adsb.fi/
adsb.fi Open Data is for personal, non-commercial use only. No licensing, selling, renting or leasing data or service. Provided without warranty; attribution and a homepage link are required. Access may be terminated. Terms: https://github.com/adsbfi/opendata/blob/main/README.md
Reference data and known callsign routes: adsbdb, https://www.adsbdb.com/
Photos: airport-data via adsbdb HTTPS image URLs. Copyright remains with the respective photographers. No photographer name is supplied by this API. Route data rights: David Taylor and Jim Mason; do not copy, publish or incorporate the route database elsewhere without explicit permission. Review all data and photo rights before publishing or commercial use.
Map snapshots: Apple Maps. Map loading makes requests to Apple for the displayed area.

Coverage is incomplete. Aircraft with no coordinates or positions more than 90 seconds old are excluded from current results. Maximum search radius is 250 NM (463 km), not a global feed. Altitude is barometric, with geometric altitude as fallback, converted to metres; it is not height above the ground. Ground speed is km/h; distance is horizontal great-circle distance. Routes are reference lookups, not verified current flight plans or actual flight tracks. Airline and registered owner are different values.

Privacy: location while in use only, no continuous background location. Search coordinates rounded to three decimal places are sent to ADSB.lol, or adsb.fi during a failure. adsbdb receives aircraft hex identifiers and callsigns. Photo services receive ordinary image requests without location coordinates supplied by SkyNear. Apple receives map-area requests. Services can see the IP address. No backend, account, analytics or embedded credentials. Routes and photos are cached in memory only; the widget disk cache contains positions and the search centre, without routes or photos.

Active app requests roughly every 20 seconds; source cooldowns and Retry-After extend this. App and extension use separate sandboxes and rate control. Both directly registered static widgets share their extension's service and currently use German. The app language selection is retained; the Live Activity follows it. No App Group or App Intents metadata processor is required. Widget loading has an overall deadline and preserves available positions when additional details time out. The current widget disk cache keeps the five displayed aircraft only, without routes or photos.

Prediction only follows a failure or cooldown of both position services: straight-line great-circle projection from a real coordinate, position time, ground speed and track, up to 15 minutes after that measurement. No ground aircraft or invalid/missing data. Orange Estimated and ~ identify predictions; altitude stays at its last measurement. No invented turns, landings or altitude changes. Successful empty responses clear the corresponding search area. Old cache files without real position times cannot support prediction.

Widgets request updates roughly every five minutes. Predicted timeline entries and an expiry entry are prepared, but iOS controls rendering and refresh timing. Location failures may show an explicitly outdated position-only cache up to 30 minutes old. Live Activities are updated by the running app; no guaranteed background projection or continuous updates without APNs/backend. Real data becomes stale after 60 seconds; estimates are marked stale by the earliest expiry of displayed predictions. System timing is not guaranteed. Compact Dynamic Island uses an actual type code, or — when unknown. Not for navigation, air traffic control or safety-critical decisions.
